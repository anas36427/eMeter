\chapter{API Endpoints Reference}
% ============================================================

\begin{infobox}
All API routes are prefixed with \texttt{/api/} in the Django project configuration. All requests expecting JSON must set the \texttt{Content-Type: application/json} header.
\end{infobox}

\section{System Health}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\ttfamily}l c X}
\toprule
\textbf{Endpoint} & \textbf{Method} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
/api/health/ & \httpget & Health check endpoint used for uptime monitoring (e.g., Render). Returns a simple JSON status. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\section{Authentication \& Session}
These endpoints manage user sessions for Administrators and Meter Readers.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\ttfamily}l c X}
\toprule
\textbf{Endpoint} & \textbf{Method} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
/api/login/ & \httppost & Authenticates a user (Admin/Reader) and issues a JWT token. \\
/api/me/ & \httpget & Retrieves the profile details of the currently authenticated user. \\
/api/profile/update/ & \httpput & Updates the profile information of the authenticated user. \\
/api/logout/ & \httppost & Logs out the current user and invalidates the session token. \\
/api/csrf/ & \httpget & Retrieves a CSRF token for securing subsequent state-changing requests. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\section{Dashboard Statistics \& Reports}
Endpoints serving aggregated data for the administrative dashboard.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\ttfamily\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.35\textwidth} c X}
\toprule
\textbf{Endpoint} & \textbf{Method} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
/api/dashboard-stats/ & \httpget & Fetches high-level KPIs (Total Revenue, Pending Dues, Total Consumers, etc.) for dashboard display. \\
/api/reports-data/ & \httpget & Retrieves detailed aggregated data used to render charts and financial reports. \\
/api/settings/ & \httpget & Retrieves the global \texttt{BillingSettings} (unit rates, duty \%, fixed charges, meter rents). \\
/api/settings/update/ & \httppost / \httpput & Updates the global billing tariff policies. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\section{Consumer Management}
Endpoints for managing the lifecycle of consumer records.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\ttfamily\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.4\textwidth} c X}
\toprule
\textbf{Endpoint} & \textbf{Method} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
/api/consumers/ & \httpget / \httppost & Lists all consumers (\texttt{GET}) or registers a new consumer (\texttt{POST}). \\
/api/consumers/<id>/ \newline /api/consumer/<id>/ & \httpget / \httpput / \httpdelete & Retrieves, updates, or soft-deletes a specific consumer by ID. \\
/api/consumers/search/ & \httpget & Searches for consumers by name, consumer number, or meter number. \\
/api/consumer/<id>/readings/ & \httpget & Fetches the historical meter readings for a specific consumer. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\section{Meter Reading \& Billing Core}
The primary data ingestion endpoints for field operations and bulk uploads.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\ttfamily\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.38\textwidth} c X}
\toprule
\textbf{Endpoint} & \textbf{Method} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
/api/readings/ & \httpget & Lists all recorded meter readings in the system. \\
/api/readings/import-excel/ & \httppost & Processes a bulk upload of \texttt{.xlsx} files, generating bills for multiple readings in batch. \\
/api/submit-reading/ & \httppost & Submits a single meter reading into the system without automatically generating a bill. \\
/api/reading-and-bill/ & \httppost & Submits a reading and atomically triggers the billing calculation engine to generate a bill. Used heavily by the mobile app sync. \\
/api/edit-reading/<id>/ & \httppatch / \httpput & Edits an existing meter reading (restricted to readings submitted on the current day). \\
/api/calculate-estimate/ & \httppost & Returns a live billing calculation estimate without saving to DB. Used for mobile app previews. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\section{Bill Management}
Endpoints for managing generated invoices and their payment lifecycles.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\ttfamily\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.38\textwidth} c X}
\toprule
\textbf{Endpoint} & \textbf{Method} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
/api/bills/ & \httpget & Lists all generated bills with pagination and filtering options. \\
/api/bill/<id>/ & \httpget & Retrieves comprehensive details for a specific generated bill. \\
/api/bill/<id>/pdf/ & \httpget & Downloads the specified bill rendered as a print-ready PDF document. \\
/api/bills/<id>/mark-paid/ & \httppost & Updates the status of a specific bill to "Paid". \\
/api/bills/<id>/mark-unpaid/ & \httppost & Reverts the status of a specific bill to "Unpaid". \\
/api/bills/manual-generate/ & \httppost & Manually forces the generation of a bill for a consumer based on existing data. \\
/api/send-bill-sms/ & \httppost & Triggers a notification (SMS/WhatsApp) to send a bill link to the consumer. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
